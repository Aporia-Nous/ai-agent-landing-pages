FAQ

Common questions

Does it have to be a phone agent?

No. Many builds never talk on the phone. They sort email, move data between tools, send reports, and post team chat alerts. Voice is there when you need it.

What counts as a workflow?

One job the agent does from start to finish. For example: answer calls and book, sort new email and alert the team, or send new form leads to your CRM with a follow-up.

Will it send messages or act without me knowing?

Only what we agree on. During testing nothing goes out, and you approve every action the agent is allowed to take.

Do I need to learn a new platform?

No. I set it up and maintain it for you.
