FAQ

Common questions

Does it only ask happy customers for reviews?

No. Everyone gets the review link, because the major review sites don't allow asking only happy customers. Unhappy customers also get a direct line to you, so you can make it right.

Can it post or answer reviews for me?

No. It asks for reviews and flags problems. You stay in control of your public replies.

Can it send texts?

Yes, once your number is registered for business texting. US carriers require this, and I handle it during setup. Until it's approved, email and calls still work.

How does it know a job is done?

From your calendar, job software, a spreadsheet, or a tag. Whatever you already use to mark work as finished.
