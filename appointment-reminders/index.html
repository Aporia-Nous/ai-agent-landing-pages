FAQ

Common questions

What kinds of businesses is this for?

Clinics, salons, med spas, auto shops, tutors, and any business that runs on appointments.

Does it work with my booking system?

It works with online calendars that sync with the major calendar apps, and it can connect to booking systems that have an API. I'll check yours before we start.

Can it send text reminders?

Yes, once your number is registered for business texting. US carriers require this, and I handle it during setup. Until it's approved, calls and emails still go out.

Can I set different timing for different services?

Yes. Reminder timing can be set per service, and a second language can be added.

Can I add this to an AI front desk?

Yes. It pairs well with an AI front desk or receptionist agent.
