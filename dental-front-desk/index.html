FAQ

Common questions

Is this HIPAA compliant?

The standard build is not. It's designed for questions, routing, and scheduling with minimal details. If the agent will handle patient health information, ask me about a HIPAA-ready build: no patient data is handled until business associate agreements (BAAs) are signed with every vendor involved, including me. Check with your compliance advisor either way.

Will it give medical advice?

No. It answers office questions only. Anything clinical or urgent goes to your staff.

Does it connect to my practice software?

It books on a calendar that syncs with the major online calendars. A direct connection to your practice software is possible if it offers an API. I'll check yours before we start.

What happens with emergencies and after-hours calls?

You set the rules. Urgent callers can be transferred to an on-call number or given your emergency instructions.
