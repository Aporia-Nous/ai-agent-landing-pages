FAQ

Common questions

Is it legal to call leads with AI?

Yes, when the lead has given consent to be called. US rules treat AI voices like recorded calls, so your form needs a consent checkbox. I help you add one, and the agent only calls during allowed hours and honors do-not-call requests. This isn't legal advice.

Does it work with my CRM?

If your CRM has an API or accepts leads by email, yes. Most popular real estate CRMs do. I'll confirm yours before we start.

Will leads know it's AI?

It speaks naturally, and if someone asks, it says it's an assistant for your team. Being honest is better for your brand and with the rules.

Can it answer calls about my listings too?

Yes. It can also answer inbound calls about your listings and book showings from them.

Can it send texts too?

Yes, once your number is registered for business texting. US carriers require this, and I handle it during setup. Until it's approved, everything else works.
