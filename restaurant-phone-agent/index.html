FAQ

Common questions

Does it work with my ordering system?

If your ordering or POS system has an API, and most modern ones do, yes. I connect through a small adapter, so you're not locked to one vendor. If yours has no API, orders go to your staff to enter.

Is taking payment this way safe?

Yes. The agent never takes card numbers by voice and stops callers who start reading one. Payment happens on your payment provider's own secure page, through a link it texts after the caller agrees.

Can it send texts?

Yes, once your number is registered for business texting. US carriers require this, and I handle it during setup. Approval usually takes a few days, sometimes a couple of weeks. Until then, everything else works.

Can it speak another language?

Yes. A second language, such as Spanish, can be added to your agent.

What if my system goes down?

The order goes to your staff instead, so nothing gets lost.
